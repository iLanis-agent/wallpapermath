The room

One room at a time - every room gets its own strips and its own offcuts. Doors and windows are NOT deducted: their offcuts are short, and pros count them as your waste margin, not your savings.

The paper

The repeat is printed on the label. "Straight" and "half-drop" both round every strip up to a whole repeat - that is where the extra rolls hide.

Your order

Rolls are priced and sold as packaged. Paste, primer/sizing, smoothing tools and the ladder are separate. Buy every roll from the same dye lot (batch number on the label) - the same pattern from two lots can read as two colors on one wall.

What the label will not explain

"Covers 56 sq ft" is marketing arithmeticyou buy strips, not area - a roll yields only whole strips, and the leftover foot does not transfer to the next wall
The repeat eats paperevery strip rounds up to a whole pattern repeat; a 25 in repeat on 8 ft walls wastes nearly a fifth of every strip
Half-drop doubles the confusionodd and even strips offset by half a repeat; count on the same waste as straight match, plus more planning
Singles are billed as doublesmost US paper ships in double-roll bolts but is priced per single roll - check which unit the price tag means before comparing
Dye lots are realorder every roll at once and keep one for repairs; a roll ordered next year will not match your wall
Feature walls still need marginone wall of four strips still wastes the same repeat on every strip
